You are a senior SaaS conversion copywriter and UX strategist.

Your task is to completely rewrite the homepage content for my product: MealCoreAI.

## PRODUCT CONTEXT

MealCoreAI is an AI-powered health diet platform that creates personalized Indian meal plans based on specific health conditions like PCOS, diabetes, thyroid, weight loss, and pregnancy.

It includes:

* AI meal planning
* AI diet assistant (chat)
* Daily guidance
* Grocery lists
* Progress tracking

## GOAL

Rewrite the homepage to maximize:

* Conversion rate (free → paid)
* User activation (starting plan generation)
* Perceived value (health outcome, not features)

## TARGET USER

* Indian users (18–45)
* People struggling with PCOS, diabetes, thyroid, weight issues
* Not looking for recipes — looking for RESULTS

---

## CRITICAL RULES (DO NOT IGNORE)

1. DO NOT position this as a “meal planner app”
2. ALWAYS focus on outcomes:

   * control blood sugar
   * reduce PCOS symptoms
   * lose weight
3. Use emotional + logical triggers
4. Make user feel urgency and trust
5. Avoid generic AI buzzwords
6. Keep language simple, direct, and benefit-driven

---

## STRUCTURE TO FOLLOW

### 1. HERO SECTION

* Headline focused on outcome (NOT features)
* Subheadline explaining how it works in 1 line
* Strong CTA (action-driven)
* Social proof line (users/results)

---

### 2. PROBLEM SECTION

* Describe user frustration:

  * confusion about diet
  * conflicting advice
  * no consistency
* Make it relatable and emotional

---

### 3. SOLUTION SECTION

* Explain how the app solves it
* Show transformation (before → after)

---

### 4. HOW IT WORKS (3 STEPS)

Each step must include:

* action
* benefit/outcome

---

### 5. CONDITION-BASED SECTION

Highlight:

* PCOS
* Diabetes
* Thyroid
* Weight loss

For each:

* one clear benefit/outcome

---

### 6. FEATURES → BENEFITS SECTION

Convert features into real-life benefits:

* meal plans → save time + better health
* AI chat → instant answers
* grocery list → convenience
* tracking → visible progress

---

### 7. SOCIAL PROOF

* Testimonials rewritten to highlight results
* Use measurable outcomes (e.g. sugar levels, weight, symptoms)

---

### 8. PRICING SECTION (IMPORTANT)

Structure:

* Free → limited (tease value)
* ₹199 → starter
* ₹499 → core
* ₹999 → premium

Each tier must:

* highlight upgrade reason
* show value clearly

---

### 9. FINAL CTA SECTION

* Strong emotional close
* Encourage immediate action

---
