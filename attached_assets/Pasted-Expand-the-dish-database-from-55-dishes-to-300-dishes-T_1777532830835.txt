When deciding which condition flags to assign, follow this logic. PCOS safe means low glycaemic index, high protein, high fibre, anti-inflammatory — so all lentils, ragi, jowar, bajra dishes, leafy greens, and grilled proteins qualify. Diabetes safe follows the same logic but also excludes high starch dishes. Thyroid safe means avoiding raw goitrogenic vegetables like raw cabbage and broccoli, but cooked versions are fine — also flag iodine rich seafood positively. Pregnancy safe means no papaya, no raw eggs, no high mercury fish, and flag iron and folate rich dishes like palak, rajma, and dals positively. Kids safe means mild spice, balanced nutrition, and under 25 minutes to prepare. Gym safe means high protein and lean — eggs, paneer, chicken, dal, sprouts all qualify. Cholesterol safe means no deep frying, no saturated fat, high fibre — oats, beans, fish, and mustard oil dishes qualify.
